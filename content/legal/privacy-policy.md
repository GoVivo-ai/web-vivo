## 1. Scope and our two roles

Vivo handles personal information in two different capacities. Which one applies depends on how your information reaches us.

**When Vivo decides how your information is used.** This Privacy Policy applies in full when you:

- Visit govivo.ai or any page we operate
- Fill out a form, book a call, or subscribe to our communications
- Receive emails, calls, or text messages from Vivo
- Apply to work with Vivo
- Represent a company that is a Vivo client, prospect, or vendor
- Hold a user account in the Vivo MarTech platform

**When Vivo processes information on behalf of a client.** Vivo provides marketing, sales, and operations teams to U.S. businesses. As part of that work, we may run advertising campaigns in our clients' own accounts, manage the leads those campaigns generate inside the Vivo MarTech platform, and contact those leads using our clients' phone and messaging systems. In these cases, the client decides why and how your information is used, and Vivo acts only as a service provider following the client's instructions. **The client's privacy policy governs that information.** If you responded to an advertisement or form from one of our clients, please contact that business directly. If you contact Vivo instead, we will forward your request to the client within ten (10) business days and let you know we have done so.

Vivo does not sell information we process on behalf of clients, and we do not use it for our own marketing.

## 4. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies in three categories: strictly necessary, analytics, and marketing. **Analytics and marketing cookies only load after you accept them.** You can accept all, reject all, or choose by category in the banner, and change your choice at any time using the **Cookie Settings** or **Your Privacy Choices** link at the bottom of every page.

We honor **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signals. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as a refusal of marketing cookies and an opt-out of sharing for advertising.

The tools we use include Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity, the Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API, and the LinkedIn Insight Tag. Full details are in our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy).

**Phone numbers are never shared with advertising platforms.** Vivo does not upload, match, or send phone numbers to Meta, LinkedIn, Google, or any other advertising platform, for any purpose. If you have accepted marketing cookies, we may send an encrypted (hashed) version of your email address to Meta or LinkedIn to measure whether our ads led to your inquiry. Job application data is never shared with advertising platforms.

## 5. Text messages

If you opt in, Vivo may send you text messages about your inquiry, scheduled calls, service updates, and occasional information about our services. Message frequency varies, typically 2 to 6 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out at any time or **HELP** for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

**No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.** Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third party, except with the service providers that deliver our messages on our behalf.

See our [SMS Terms](/sms-terms) for full details.

## 6. How we share your information

**Vivo does not sell your personal information for money.**

We share personal information only in these cases:

- **Service providers** who help us run our business under contracts that limit their use of the information to the services they provide to us. These include website hosting, analytics, advertising measurement, email delivery, scheduling, telephone and text messaging, cloud storage, and customer relationship management. - **Advertising platforms**, only as described in Section 4 and only after you accept marketing cookies. Under some U.S. state laws this counts as "sharing" for targeted advertising. You can refuse or withdraw it at any time.
- **Legal and safety reasons**, when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Vivo, our clients, or others.
- **Business transfers**, if Vivo is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this Privacy Policy.
- **With your consent**, in any other case you authorize.

## 8. How we protect your information

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including:

- Multi-factor authentication on internal systems
- Role-based access, so team members only see what their work requires
- Background checks for team members before they receive access to systems
- Written confidentiality agreements signed by every team member
- Encryption of information in transit

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a security incident that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as required by applicable law.

## 10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **Know and access** the personal information we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate information
- **Delete** your personal information
- **Receive a copy** of your information in a portable format
- **Opt out** of the sharing of your information for targeted advertising
- **Withdraw consent** you have given us, such as for text messages or marketing emails
- **Appeal** a decision we make about your request

**We extend these rights to every person, regardless of the state or country where you live.**

**How to make a request.** Email **privacy@govivo.ai** or call **(385) 342-5646**. We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we will tell you why, and we may extend by up to 45 additional days. To protect your information, we will verify your identity by matching details you provide with the information we hold. You may use an authorized agent, who must provide proof of your written permission.

**Appeals.** If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject line "Appeal." We will respond within 45 days.

**Non-discrimination.** We will not deny you services, charge you a different price, or provide a different level of service because you exercised your rights.

**Marketing communications.** You can unsubscribe from our emails using the link in any message, and stop text messages by replying STOP.
