## Not Vivo subprocessors

Telephony and messaging providers contracted directly by a customer, such as RingCentral or Dialpad under the customer's own account, are the customer's providers, not Vivo's subprocessors. Vivo teams operate within those accounts following the customer's instructions.

Advertising platforms where campaigns run in a customer's own ad account are likewise the customer's relationship, not a Vivo subprocessor relationship.
