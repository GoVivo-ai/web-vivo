# Cookie Policy

**Go Vivo Inc.**
Last updated: September 2026
Version 1.0

This Cookie Policy explains how Go Vivo Inc. ("Vivo," "we," "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on govivo.ai. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

---

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include pixels, tags, and scripts that collect information about how you use a website. In this policy we call all of them "cookies."

---

## 2. Your choice comes first

When you first visit our website, a banner lets you **Accept all**, **Reject all**, or **Customize** your choice by category. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.

**Only strictly necessary cookies load before you choose.** Analytics and marketing cookies stay blocked until you accept them.

You can change your choice at any time using the **Cookie Settings** link at the bottom of every page. If your browser sends a **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signal, we treat it as a refusal of marketing cookies.

---

## 3. Categories of cookies we use

### Strictly necessary

These cookies make the website work and remember your cookie choices. They cannot be turned off, and they do not track you across other websites.

| Provider | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vivo (consent manager) | Stores your cookie choices so we respect them on every visit | 1 year |
| Vivo (website hosting and security) | Protects the site against abuse and keeps forms working | Session |

### Analytics

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it. They load only if you accept them.

| Provider | Tool | Purpose | Typical duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google | Google Analytics 4 | Counts visits, pages viewed, and traffic sources | Up to 2 years |
| Microsoft | Clarity | Shows how visitors scroll and click, using recordings in which the content of form fields is always hidden | Up to 1 year |

### Marketing

These cookies help us measure whether our advertising works and show relevant ads on other platforms. They load only if you accept them.

| Provider | Tool | Purpose | Typical duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meta | Meta Pixel and Conversions API | Measures whether visits and inquiries came from our ads on Facebook and Instagram | Up to 90 days |
| LinkedIn | LinkedIn Insight Tag | Measures whether visits and inquiries came from our ads on LinkedIn | Up to 1 year |

Google Tag Manager is the tool we use to load and control these technologies. It does not set cookies of its own and only activates each tool according to your choice.

The complete and current list of cookies, with their exact names and durations, is always available in the **Cookie Settings** panel.

---

## 4. What we never do with this information

- We never send phone numbers to advertising platforms.
- We never share job application information with advertising platforms.
- The Vivo MarTech platform at platform.govivo.ai does not use advertising cookies. It uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and secure.

---

## 5. Controlling cookies in your browser

In addition to our banner, you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the website works. You can also use:

- Google Analytics opt-out: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
- Meta ad preferences: accountscenter.facebook.com
- LinkedIn ad preferences: linkedin.com/psettings/advertising

---

## 6. Changes to this policy

When we add or remove tools, we update this policy and the Cookie Settings panel, and change the "Last updated" date above. If a change adds a new category of cookies, we will ask for your consent again.
